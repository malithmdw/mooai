# On-Call Handbook — Incident Response Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This runbook defines the standard incident response process for all Novus Bank production incidents. All on-call engineers must read this document before taking their first shift.

## 2. Severity Classification

| Severity | Definition | Response Time | War-Room |
|----------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| P1 | Customer-facing outage or payment processing stopped | 5 min page acknowledgement | Mandatory |
| P2 | Significant degradation, SLO breach imminent | 15 min acknowledgement | If > 30 min |
| P3 | Minor degradation, SLO not at risk | Next business day | No |
| P4 | Informational | Weekly review | No |

## 3. P1 Response Procedure

### Step 1: Acknowledge
- Acknowledge PagerDuty alert within 5 minutes
- Join `#incident-p1` Slack channel (auto-created by PagerDuty)

### Step 2: Assess
- Check Grafana payment gateway dashboard: `grafana.internal/d/pmt-gateway`
- Identify affected systems from alert tags
- Determine customer impact scope

### Step 3: Declare and Communicate
- Post in `#incident-p1`: "P1 DECLARED — [brief description] — IC: [your name]"
- Notify engineering lead if payment processing is stopped

### Step 4: Mitigate
- Prioritise customer impact reduction over root cause investigation
- Use service pause flags (see RUN-002) to stop bleeding
- Escalate to specialists (DBA, security, payments) as needed

### Step 5: Resolve
- Confirm resolution with monitoring data (not just absence of errors)
- Post resolution summary in `#incident-p1`
- Transition to post-incident review

## 4. Post-Incident Review

All P1 and P2 incidents require a written incident report within 5 business days (see template in data/knowledge/incidents/). The report must include:
- Timeline of events
- Root cause analysis
- Contributing factors
- Impact quantification
- Remediation actions with JIRA tickets

## 5. Escalation Matrix

| Situation | Escalate To |
|-----------|-------------|
| Payment processing stopped > 10 min | Head of Payments Engineering |
| Suspected security breach | CISO and Security team immediately |
| Customer data exposure | DPO and Legal immediately |
| Regulatory breach likely | Compliance team within 30 minutes |
