# Novus Premier Account — Product Specification

## 1. Product Overview

The Novus Premier Account is a premium retail current account targeting high-net-worth individuals. It includes international payment capabilities, dedicated support, and premium card benefits.

## 2. Account Features

| Feature | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Monthly fee | £25 |
| Eligibility | Income > £75,000 or savings > £100,000 with Novus |
| Debit card | Visa Infinite (metal card) |
| Credit card | Novus Premier Rewards Card (separate agreement) |
| Airport lounge access | Priority Pass (unlimited visits) |
| Travel insurance | Comprehensive, worldwide |
| Dedicated relationship manager | Named RM, direct phone line |

## 3. Payment Capabilities

| Payment Type | Limit |
|---|---|
| Faster Payments | £1,000,000 per transaction; £2,000,000 per day |
| CHAPS | Unlimited |
| SWIFT International | Unlimited |
| Contactless | £100 per tap |

## 4. International Features

- SWIFT international transfers: £15 per payment (waived for balances > £250,000)
- Preferential FX rate: mid-market rate + 0.25% (vs 0.5% standard)
- Zero foreign transaction fee on debit card

## 5. Eligibility Criteria

- Minimum annual income of £75,000 (verified via Open Banking income data or payslips); OR
- Minimum savings/investment balance of £100,000 held with Novus
- Pass standard KYC and credit check
- UK resident

## 6. Regulatory Notes

- Payment account regulated under Payment Accounts Regulations 2015
- Premium features (travel insurance, airport lounge) are non-regulated benefits
- FSCS protection applies to deposit balances up to £85,000
