## 3. Bank Holiday Threshold Procedure

Agreed procedure (to be added to RUN-009):

On bank holiday long weekends (Friday before through Tuesday after):
- Monitor CNP false positive rate from Friday 08:00 UTC
- If rate exceeds 0.8% for 15 minutes: proactively raise threshold to 0.85
- Notify Michelle Torres and the on-call fraud analyst immediately
- Review and revert to 0.7 within 24 hours of holiday period ending

Ben: "The model retrain should eliminate the need for this manual override in most cases, but the procedure is a good safety net."
