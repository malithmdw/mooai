# Sanctions & AML Technology Review — Q2 2024

**Date**: 2024-04-25  
**Location**: Secure conference room B2  
**Chair**: Fatima Al-Hassan (Chief Compliance Officer)  
**Attendees**: David Chen (CISO), Sanjay Kapoor (Compliance Technology Lead), Priya Sharma (Payments Engineering), MLRO (attending as observer)

---

## Agenda

1. Review of sanctions incidents Q1 2024
2. Sanctions screening service reliability concerns
3. SDN list refresh process improvements
4. Regulatory position update

---

## 1. Sanctions Incidents Q1 2024

Fatima opened by noting two significant sanctions technology incidents in Q1 2024:

**INC-2024-005** (March): OOM crash in sanctions-screening service halted all payments for 61 minutes. Hotfix deployed. Streaming parser now used.

**INC-2024-010** (June): Partial SDN list served for 52 minutes following failed migration. 1,240 payments required re-screening.

Fatima: "Two incidents of this nature in 4 months is not acceptable given the legal exposure of a sanctions breach. The technology team must treat the sanctions screening service with the same reliability standard as the payment gateway itself."

## 2. Sanctions Screening Reliability

Sanjay presented a reliability analysis:
- Sanctions-screening achieved 99.87% availability in Q1 (against 99.99% target)
- Two incidents account for 113 minutes of downtime
- Root causes: infrastructure (OOM) and process (migration without rollback data integrity check)

**Proposed actions**:
1. Move sanctions-screening to dedicated high-memory node pool (4 GB minimum)
2. Add pre- and post-migration SDN entry count assertion (JIRA COMP-522)
3. Implement blue-green deployment for database migrations

David Chen raised the supply chain concern: the OFAC list format change (prior to INC-2024-005) was not pre-announced. "We need better intelligence on when major list format changes are coming. Can we get early access via the OFAC API partner programme?"

Action: Sanjay to investigate OFAC partner programme for early notification.

## 3. SDN List Refresh Process

Agreed changes to the refresh process:
- Post-load entry count validation (mandatory gate, blocks activation if out of range)
- Streaming parser for all list formats (implemented post-INC-2024-005)
- Blue-green refresh: new list version loaded in parallel; switch only on validation pass

## 4. Regulatory Position

MLRO confirmed:
- Both incidents reported to FCA and OFSI as required
- No enforcement action for either incident
- FCA has requested quarterly reporting on sanctions technology resilience for remainder of 2024
