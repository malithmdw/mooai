# SWIFT Gateway DR Post-Incident Review — INC-2024-002

**Date**: 2024-02-08  
**Location**: Secure boardroom (restricted attendance)  
**Chair**: Priya Sharma (Head of Payments Engineering)  
**Attendees**: David Chen (CISO), Fatima Al-Hassan (Compliance), James Whitfield (Platform), Aisha Okonkwo (SRE), Legal representative

---

## Purpose

Post-incident review for INC-2024-002 (SWIFT MT103 double-submission, GBP 4.2 M duplicates, 38 payments recalled).

---

## Incident Summary

Priya presented the INC-2024-002 timeline (see full incident report). Key facts:
- 38 MT103 messages doubled during DR failover on 2024-02-03
- Root cause: DR node lacked Redis idempotency store connectivity
- All 38 duplicates successfully recalled via SWIFT gpi
- Regulatory notification filed with FCA

## Technical Root Cause Discussion

James confirmed the DR failover design has a documented gap: the DR runbook (RUN-006) did not include a Redis connectivity pre-check. The DR test conducted in Q4 2023 identified this gap but it was not tracked to completion in JIRA.

**Aisha**: "This is the same architectural weakness as our idempotency design in ARCH-004 — async Redis replication plus a gap in our DR pre-checks created the conditions for this incident."

Agreed: The DR runbook must be updated before the next maintenance window. Redis cross-DC replication must be synchronous for idempotency-critical paths.

## Regulatory Discussion

Fatima confirmed FCA notification was filed within the 24-hour window. The FCA has acknowledged and is monitoring the remediation plan. No enforcement action at this time.

Fatima raised concern: "We have now had two incidents this month that required regulatory notification. The pattern of idempotency failures needs to be resolved systematically, not just incident by incident."
