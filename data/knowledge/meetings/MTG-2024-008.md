# Technology Risk Committee — Q3 2024

**Date**: 2024-09-05  
**Location**: Board Room, London HQ  
**Chair**: CRO (Chief Risk Officer)  
**Attendees**: CTO, CISO (David Chen), CCO (Fatima Al-Hassan), Head of Payments Engineering (Priya Sharma), Board Technology Committee Observer

---

## Agenda

1. Q3 technology risk summary

4. Q4 risk appetite and remediation priorities

---

## 1. Q3 Technology Risk Summary

CRO opened by noting the elevated technology risk profile in H1 2024. Key statistics:
- 8 P1 incidents in H1 2024 (vs 4 in H1 2023) — 100% increase
- 5 of 8 incidents required regulatory notification
- Total estimated revenue impact: GBP 1.1 M
- Largest single incident: INC-2024-002 (SWIFT duplicates, GBP 4.2 M in recalled payments)

CTO: "The incident volume is unacceptable. We have a concentrated reliability problem in the payments infrastructure, and we've committed to the board that we'll reduce P1 payment incidents to ≤ 3 per half-year by Q1 2025."

## 2. Recurring Root Cause Themes

Priya presented a consolidated analysis of all H1 2024 payment incidents:

**Theme 1: Certificate management** — 2 incidents (INC-2024-001, INC-2024-004)  
Remediation: POL-005 published; cert-renewal alerting improved. Risk reducing.

**Theme 2: Idempotency store failures** — 3 incidents (INC-2024-002, INC-2024-007, INC-2024-011)  
Remediation: Architecture redesign in progress (see MTG-2024-005). PostgreSQL migration for high-value payments targeted Q4 2024. Risk: MEDIUM — design change in flight but not yet delivered.

**Theme 3: Sanctions screening infrastructure** — 2 incidents (INC-2024-005, INC-2024-010)  
Remediation: Streaming parser deployed; migration count assertion added. Risk: MEDIUM — blue-green deployment not yet implemented.

**Theme 4: Dependency/library version mismatches** — 2 incidents (INC-2024-007, INC-2024-014)  
Remediation: Payment-path regression tests mandated; transitive dependency scanning in progress. Risk: MEDIUM.

## 3. Regulatory Exposure

Fatima summarised the regulatory position:
- FCA is monitoring Novus Bank's operational resilience following 5 notifications in H1 2024
- PRA supervision team has requested an operational resilience self-assessment by 2024-11-01
- No enforcement actions, but FCA has indicated supervisory concern about payment system reliability
- Key deadline: PRA DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) implementation review — March 2025

David: "The volume of regulatory notifications is our biggest concern from a supervisory relationship perspective. Every notification is a data point that regulators use to assess our overall risk profile."

## 4. Q4 Risk Appetite and Priorities

Agreed top 3 technology risk priorities for Q4 2024:
1. **Idempotency store redesign** — deliver PostgreSQL migration for CHAPS/SWIFT by 2024-12-01
2. **PRA DORA readiness** — self-assessment by 2024-11-01; remediation roadmap by 2024-12-01
3. **Payment path test coverage** — achieve 80% coverage on payment-critical code paths by 2024-11-30
