# Payment Operations Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the operational standards for all payment processing at Novus Bank, including scheme compliance, error handling, and incident escalation.

## 2. Payment Scheme Membership

Novus Bank holds direct membership in:
- **Faster Payments Scheme (FPS)**: via Vocalink (technical aggregator)
- **Bacs Payment Schemes Limited**: Direct submission
- **CHAPS**: Via the Bank of England RTGS infrastructure
- **SWIFT**: BIC: NOVBGB2L

Scheme rules take precedence over internal policies. Any conflict must be escalated to the Payments Compliance team.

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Payment Controls

No payment may be submitted to a scheme without passing ALL of the following:

1. **Sanctions screening** — synchronous clearance via sanctions-screening service
2. **Fraud score** — score below threshold or manual review approved
3. **Idempotency check** — no duplicate submission
4. **Balance sufficiency** — available balance confirmed
5. **Authorisation** — customer or authorised agent has approved payment

Bypassing any control requires written approval from the Head of Payments Engineering and Chief Compliance Officer.

## 4. Payment Limits

See individual product specifications (PROD-001, PROD-002, PROD-007) for per-product limits. Technical limits enforced by the payment gateway:

| Scheme | Hard Technical Limit |
|--------|---------------------|
| FPS | £1,000,000 per transaction |
| CHAPS | No limit (RTGS) |
| SWIFT | No limit |
| Bacs DD | No limit |

## 5. Duplicate Payment Response

In the event of confirmed duplicate payments:
1. Activate HOLD_PAYMENTS circuit immediately
2. Declare P1 incident
3. Reverse or recall all confirmed duplicates within 4 hours
4. Notify all affected customers within 2 hours
5. File regulatory notification if total duplicate value > £1 M or > 100 transactions

> Reference incidents: INC-2024-002 (SWIFT), INC-2024-007 (CHAPS), INC-2024-011 (Redis failover)

## 6. Scheme Incident Reporting

| Scheme | Reporting Obligation |
|--------|---------------------|
| CHAPS | Bank of England: notify within 2 hours of P1 affecting CHAPS |
| FPS | Vocalink: notify via scheme operations portal |
| SWIFT | SWIFT CISO team for security incidents |
| Bacs | Bacs scheme operations: notify within 24 hours |
