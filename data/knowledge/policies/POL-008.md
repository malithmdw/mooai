# Sanctions Compliance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines Novus Bank's obligations and standards for compliance with international financial sanctions regimes. Non-compliance with sanctions is a criminal offence.

## 2. Applicable Sanctions Regimes

Novus Bank screens against all of the following:

| Regime | Authority | List Name |
|--------|----------|----------|
| US OFAC | US Treasury | Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) |
| UK HMT | His Majesty's Treasury | UK Consolidated Sanctions List |
| UN | United Nations | UN Consolidated List |
| EU | European Union | EU Consolidated Financial Sanctions List |

## 3. Mandatory Screening Requirements

**All outbound payments** must pass sanctions screening before submission. No payment may be released without a CLEARED result from the sanctions-screening service (see ARCH-006).

Exceptions: There are NO exceptions. If the sanctions-screening service is unavailable, the payment gateway must activate HOLD_PAYMENTS. Payments must not be processed against a cached or partial list without Compliance Officer authorisation.

> **Incident reference**: INC-2024-005 — OOM crash in sanctions-screening caused HOLD_PAYMENTS activation and 61-minute payment halt. This was the correct system behaviour. INC-2024-010 — partial SDN list was served for 52 minutes; 1,240 payments were subsequently re-screened.

## 4. Screening Thresholds

There is no minimum threshold for sanctions screening. All payments regardless of value must be screened.

Fuzzy matching: name matching uses a Levenshtein distance of ≤ 2 characters for matches on SDN list names. Matches generate a MATCH_CANDIDATE result requiring manual review.

## 5. OFSI Licence and Derogation

Where a payment involves a sanctioned party but a licence has been obtained from OFSI (Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation), the payment may proceed. The licence reference must be:
- Documented in the payment record
- Approved by the MLRO (Money Laundering Reporting Officer)
- Retained for 7 years

## 6. Suspicious Transaction Reporting

Where a payment is blocked on sanctions grounds, a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed with the National Crime Agency (NCA) within 24 hours.
