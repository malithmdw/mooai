# GDPR and Privacy Compliance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines Novus Bank's obligations and standards for compliance with UK GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 2. Lawful Bases for Processing

| Processing Activity | Lawful Basis | Notes |
|--------------------|----|-----|
| Operating customer accounts | Contract | Necessary for account operation |
| AML/fraud screening | Legal obligation | POCA 2002, MLR 2017 |
| Marketing (opted-in) | Consent | Explicit opt-in required |
| Analytics (aggregate) | Legitimate interests | Balancing test documented |
| SAR fulfilment | Legal obligation | UK GDPR Art. 15 |

## 3. Data Subject Rights

| Right | Timeframe | System |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Subject Access Request (SAR) | 30 days | data-export-service |
| Right to Erasure | 30 days | Applies post-retention period; legal holds may delay |
| Right to Rectification | 30 days | Customer portal or branch |
| Right to Portability | 30 days | data-export-service (Open Banking format) |
| Right to Object | Immediate | Stops marketing processing |

> **Operational incident**: INC-2024-015 — SAR processing pipeline failed for 48 hours due to missing database index. 340 SARs delayed. Drives requirement for SAR system SLO monitoring and DBA review of schema changes.

## 4. Personal Data Inventory

All systems processing personal data must be registered in the Record of Processing Activities (ROPA). Engineering teams must notify the Data Protection Officer (DPO) within 5 business days of introducing a new personal data processing activity.

## 5. Privacy by Design

New products and system changes that involve personal data must undergo a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) if they:
- Process special category data
- Involve automated decision-making with significant effect
- Involve large-scale monitoring of individuals
- Introduce a new processing purpose

The AI Financial Assistant (PROD-009) is subject to DPIA before any customer-facing deployment.

## 6. Data Breach Notification

| Breach Type | Notification | Timeline |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Risk to individuals | ICO | Within 72 hours |
| No risk to individuals | Internal log | No external notification |
| Customer notification | Affected customers | Without undue delay |

The DPO must be notified immediately of any potential personal data breach for assessment.

## 7. International Data Transfers

Personal data may only be transferred outside the UK to countries with an adequacy decision or under UK Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Cloud service providers (AWS, etc.) are covered under pre-existing SCCs. New international transfers require DPO approval.
