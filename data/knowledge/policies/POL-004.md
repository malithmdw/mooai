# Document and Records Retention — Operational Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers the retention and management of operational records including cheque images, payment files, reconciliation records, and regulatory submissions.

## 2. Cheque Image Retention

| Phase | Storage | Duration | Policy |
|-------|---------|---------|--------|
| Active processing | MinIO hot tier | 90 days | Auto-lifecycle purge |
| Archive | AWS S3 Standard-IA | Up to 7 years | Manual archive job |
| Long-term | AWS S3 Glacier | > 7 years | Per legal requirements |

The 90-day hot-tier lifecycle policy must be active at all times. Any suspension (e.g., for legal hold) must be tracked and re-enabled with a formal process.

> **Incident reference**: INC-2024-013 demonstrates the consequence of an un-re-enabled lifecycle policy: quota exhaustion and clearing cycle delay.

## 3. Payment File Retention

| File Type | Retention | Storage |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Bacs AUDDIS/ADDACS files | 7 years | Secure object storage |
| SWIFT MT message logs | 7 years | SWIFT Alliance archive |
| FPS payment submissions | 7 years | Internal archive |
| CHAPS payment orders | 7 years | Internal archive |

## 4. Reconciliation Records

Daily reconciliation reports between the core-banking-ledger and scheme settlement must be retained for 7 years. Any reconciliation breaks exceeding £1,000 must be escalated to Finance within 24 hours.

## 5. Regulatory Submission Records

All regulatory reports (FCA, PRA, BoE) must be retained for 10 years with a tamper-evident hash to ensure integrity.

## 6. Legal Hold Process

1. Legal team requests hold via legal-hold API (references POL-002 legal hold procedure)
2. Affected documents tagged with `legal-hold: true` in object storage
3. Lifecycle policies paused for tagged items only (not entire bucket)
4. Legal hold reviewed quarterly; released promptly when investigation concludes
5. Release triggers: (a) lifecycle re-enable, (b) JIRA task created for tracking
