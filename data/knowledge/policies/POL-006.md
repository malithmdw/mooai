# Open Banking Compliance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines Novus Bank's obligations and standards for compliance with PSD2, the Open Banking UK Standard, and FCA regulatory requirements for Open Banking.

## 2. Regulatory Framework

| Regulation | Authority | Key Obligation |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| PSD2 Directive | EU / UK implementation | Allow authorised TPPs to access accounts and initiate payments |
| FCA RTS on SCA | FCA | Strong Customer Authentication for payment initiation |
| Open Banking UK Standard v3.1.11 | Open Banking Limited | Technical API standards |
| Payment Accounts Regulations 2015 | FCA | Non-discrimination, access rules |

## 3. TPP Access Obligations

Novus Bank must not:
- Obstruct or create unnecessary barriers to TPP access
- Apply additional authentication beyond what customers face directly
- Treat TPP-initiated payment requests differently to direct customer requests

These obligations are absolute. Any system change that could reduce TPP access must be reviewed by the Open Banking compliance team before deployment.

> **Compliance risk**: INC-2024-008 — rate limiter misconfiguration effectively blocked all 47 registered TPPs for 76 minutes. This could constitute a PSD2 breach. The incident is under compliance review.

## 4. API Availability Requirements

Under the FCA RTS, Novus Bank must:
- Publish a dedicated interface (the Open Banking API) for TPP access
- Achieve at least the same availability as the customer-facing interface
- Provide a fallback mechanism or contingency measure if the dedicated interface is unavailable

Availability target: 99.5% monthly uptime for Open Banking API (see POL-001 for SLO).

## 5. Dispute and Exception Handling

If a TPP raises a dispute about access or data accuracy:
1. Open Banking team must acknowledge within 1 business day
2. Investigation must commence within 3 business days
3. Resolution or escalation to FCA within 15 business days

## 6. Annual Compliance Review

An annual review of Open Banking compliance is required, covering:
- API conformance testing (Open Banking conformance suite)
- Security penetration test of Open Banking endpoints
- Review of all TPP access logs for anomalies
- FCA regulatory return submission (annual)
