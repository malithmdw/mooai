# Service Level Agreements and SLO Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the Service Level Objectives (SLOs) for Novus Bank's critical digital services and the process for measuring, reporting, and responding to SLO breaches.

## 2. Critical Service SLOs

### Payment Gateway

| Metric | SLO | Measurement Window |
|--------|-----|-------------------|
| Availability | 99.99% | Rolling 30 days |
| Payment submission p99 latency | < 2 s | Per minute |
| Duplicate payment rate | 0% | Per month |

### Core Banking Ledger

| Metric | SLO | Measurement Window |
|--------|-----|-------------------|
| Write availability | 99.95% | Rolling 30 days |
| Read availability | 99.9% | Rolling 30 days |
| Replication lag | < 2 minutes | Per minute |

### Mobile Banking API

| Metric | SLO | Measurement Window |
|--------|-----|-------------------|
| Availability | 99.9% | Rolling 30 days |
| p99 response time | < 500 ms | Per minute |

### Open Banking API

| Metric | SLO | Measurement Window |
|--------|-----|-------------------|
| Availability | 99.9% | Rolling 30 days |
| Uptime to TPPs | 99.5% | Per month |

## 3. Error Budget Policy

Each SLO has a corresponding error budget:
- **99.99% availability SLO** = 4.3 minutes downtime per month
- **99.9% availability SLO** = 43.8 minutes downtime per month

Error budget usage:
- 0–50% consumed: normal operations
- 50–75% consumed: engineering team notified; reduce deployment risk
- 75–90% consumed: freeze non-essential deployments; engineering lead review
- 90–100% consumed: freeze all deployments; CTO notified; post-mortem required

## 4. SLO Reporting

- Real-time SLO dashboards in Grafana (see ARCH-009)
- Weekly SLO report to engineering leads
- Monthly SLO summary to CTO and board technology committee

## 5. Regulatory Considerations

FCA SYSC sourcebook requires operational resilience targets. Payment systems must have documented impact tolerances reviewed annually. Current targets:
- **Payment gateway**: maximum tolerable disruption = 30 minutes
- **Core banking**: maximum tolerable disruption = 2 hours
