# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long Novus Bank retains customer data and how data is securely deleted after the retention period. Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all teams.

## 2. Retention Schedule

| Data Category | Retention Period | Basis |
|--------------|-----------------|-------|
| Transaction records | 7 years post-account closure | FCA COBS 9.5R |
| Customer identity (KYC) | 5 years post-account closure | MLR 2017 |
| Account statements | 7 years | FCA COBS |
| Cheque images | 90 days (hot tier); 7 years (cold archive) | Image Clearing System rules |
| Call recordings | 3 years | FCA COBS 11.8R |
| Fraud investigation records | 7 years | POCA 2002 |
| Application data (declined) | 2 years | Legitimate business interest |
| Web/app access logs | 90 days | Operational need |
| Consent records (PSD2) | 5 years post-consent expiry | PSD2 RTS |

## 3. GDPR Subject Access Requests (SAR)

- SARs must be fulfilled within 30 days of receipt (UK GDPR Art. 12)
- Extensions of up to 60 days permitted for complex requests (with notification to customer)
- SAR processing is handled by the data-export-service
- Automated system: customer data aggregated from core-banking-ledger, fraud systems, comms records
- See INC-2024-015 for an incident where SAR processing timed out due to missing database index

## 4. Data Deletion

After the retention period, data must be:
1. Identified by the retention-scheduler service
2. Reviewed for any legal holds (Legal team approval required)
3. Deleted from all primary systems within 30 days of retention expiry
4. Confirmed deleted from backups within 180 days (per backup rotation cycle)

## 5. Legal Holds

Legal holds suspend deletion for data subject to litigation, regulatory investigation, or court order. Legal holds must be:
- Applied by the Legal team via the legal-hold API
- Documented with case reference and estimated duration
- Reviewed quarterly
- Released promptly when no longer required (release triggers deletion workflow)

> **Operational note**: An unreleased legal hold on cheque images caused INC-2024-013 (quota exhaustion). Legal hold releases must include a task to re-enable lifecycle policies.
